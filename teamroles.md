## Team roles
- Each week will have the team rotate through the roles listed below
### Scrum master
- Leads the sprint planning
- Responsible for keeping backlogs up-to-date during the sprint, reminding the team to keep updating tasks and stories if necessary
- Creates a new sprint in timelogs at the change of sprints
- Lead the daily scrums
- Ensures that standards set by the team are followed
- Ensures every topic on the agenda is covered during the meeting
- Ensures everyone willing gets a turn to speak during the customer meeting
- Leads the retrospective and the pulse
### Scribe
- Prepares meeting notes from customer meetings
- Prepares the agenda for the customer meetings
- ### Demoer
- Showcases the current state of the application at customer meetings
- Has to arrive in customer meetings early enough to setup their computer and the application in advance, including potential necessary database operations
